FLAME FIGHT

HOW TO PLAY

  • ◀ / ▶ : MOVE LEFT / RIGHT
  • ▲ : JUMP
  • SPACE : SHOOT (GROUND ONLY)
  • EACH HIT COSTS 1 OF YOUR 5 HP
  • EVERY ROUND GETS HARDER THAN THE LAST
  • WIN A ROUND BY EMPTYING YOUR RIVAL'S HP FIRST
  • PAUSE, RESUME OR EXIT ANYTIME DURING A MATCH

ABOUT

FLAME FIGHT is a 2D fighting game where you face off against a computer-controlled rival. All art, sound and characters were added by the game's creator.
